- Status: open

## Outcome

The CLI client speaks to the check endpoint from E6-T3.

- A local error does not call it.
- Local success on `--dry-run` calls it, prints the resolved sentence, and does not POST a `Run`.
- Local success and no error diagnostics POST one `Run` and print its name.
- Any error diagnostic, including one returned by the server, prints the rendered diagnostic and does not POST a `Run`.
- A `pool.waiting` note still POSTs the `Run`.

Tests use a local listener or the handler in-process. They do not use a cluster.
