## Outcome

The reader accepts job name, nodes, GPUs (`--gpus`, `--gpus-per-node`, `--gres=gpu:`), CPUs, memory, time, account (mapped to `team`), and output (mapped to `into`). The script body becomes `run` as argv, not `sh -c`. Any other `#SBATCH` directive fails and quotes the line. `--array` and `--dependency` fail. CPUs and memory are kept on the spec for later rendering. They do not invent new phrases.
