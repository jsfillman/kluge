## Outcome

`mint_webhook` returns a CA, a certificate named `kluge-controller.kluge.svc` and `localhost`, and the CA as a base64 bundle with no newlines. The chart's HTTPS port is 8443. A localhost TLS listener using that certificate completes a handshake with a client that trusts the CA.
