# E19-T1 Classify a path

- Status: open
- Epic: [E19](epics/E19.md)
- Depends: E10-T1
- Spec: [storage.md](../spec/storage.md) "Hybrid"

## Outcome

`classify(spec, files, spill)` returns each path as `cache`, `object`, or `omitted`.

- Under `into`: `object`.
- Over `spill`, anywhere under the team root: `object`.
- Under `dir`, not under `into`, at or under `spill`: `cache`.
- On the team root but outside `dir` and `into`: `omitted`. A checkpoint that was not declared is not in the laptop manifest.

`spill` defaults to `1Gi` when the caller passes none.
