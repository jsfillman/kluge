## Outcome

- `into results` and no stdout path sets `KLUGE_STDOUT` and `KLUGE_STDERR` to `results/%x-%j.out` after expansion.
- A script with a stdout path or a signal uses `/kluge/launch` even when `across` is 1.
- `--signal=B:TERM@60` on a 1 hour job sets `KLUGE_SIGNAL` to `TERM` and `KLUGE_SIGNAL_AT` to `3540`. `--signal=TERM@60` is `parse.sbatch`.
- `--export=NONE` stores no names. `--export=FOO,BAR` stores those names. `fill_export` copies values from the environment it is given and skips a missing name. `--export=ALL` is unchanged.
