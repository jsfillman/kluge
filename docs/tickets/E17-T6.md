## Outcome

`KLUGE_TEAM_MAX_GPUS` is the quota `check_pool` already takes. The chart passes `team.maxGpus` into that variable. Unset means the previous unlimited quota, so a single-team install does not grow a new required setting.

A quota of 8 refuses `on 8 h100` `across 4` with `pool.capacity`. The same sentence with quota 32 is accepted when the pool has the nodes.
