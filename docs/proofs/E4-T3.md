>
> `--output` is the stdout path. `--error`, when set, is stderr. `%j` is the run id, `%x` is the name, and `%n` is the node index.
>
> `--chdir` is `dir`.
>
> `--export=ALL` is refused. The container starts from the image, plus `with`, plus the variables above.
